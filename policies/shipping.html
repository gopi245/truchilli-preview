Shipping & Delivery Policy

This Shipping Policy applies to every order placed on truchilli.com, owned and operated by The Guntur Mirchi (“Truchilli”, “we”, “us”), Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India. By placing an order you agree to the terms below.

Where we ship

We ship across India. For international orders, please message us on WhatsApp at +91 81424 24269: we will confirm the courier charge and payment before dispatch.

Shipping charges

Standard delivery within India is charged by the weight of your order:

  • Up to 1 kg: ₹99
  • 1.1 kg to 2 kg: ₹149
  • 2.1 kg to 3 kg: ₹179
  • Above 3 kg: free (launch offer, for a limited time)

The exact charge is shown in your cart before you pay. Any customs duties or import taxes on international orders are paid by the customer.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday to Saturday). You will receive an order confirmation straight away, and a shipping confirmation with the tracking details once your parcel is dispatched. Processing may take a little longer during festivals and sale periods.

Delivery time

  • Andhra Pradesh and Telangana: 3–6 business days
  • Rest of India: 5–9 business days
  • Remote or rural PIN codes may take a few days longer

These are estimates from the day of dispatch; weather, public holidays and courier delays can affect them.

Tracking your order

A tracking number and link are sent by email, SMS or WhatsApp when your order ships. You can also track it any time with your order number and phone number under Track your order. Please make sure someone is available at the address to receive the package.

Delays, damage and missing parcels

If your order is late, contact us with your order number and we will chase the courier. If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, please refuse delivery or record a video while opening it and report it to us within 2 days of delivery (see our Refund & Return Policy). We cannot be held liable for delays caused by the courier, but we will always help resolve them.
