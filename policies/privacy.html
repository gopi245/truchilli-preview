Privacy Policy

Website: https://truchilli.com

Company: The Guntur Mirchi (“Truchilli”)

Truchilli (“we”, “our” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you visit truchilli.com. By using the Website you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information we collect

Personal information

When you place an order, log in, subscribe to our emails or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address and PIN code
  • Order history and messages you send us
  • User-generated content such as reviews or review photos

Non-personal information

We may also collect non-identifying information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on pages and the website that referred you.

How we use your information

  • To process, pack, deliver and support your orders
  • To send order updates by email, SMS or WhatsApp
  • To send newsletters and offers, with your consent (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To respond to your enquiries and complaints
  • To improve the Website and our products
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues or fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations

Payments

Online payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details; they are handled on Razorpay's secure systems under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and local storage to remember your cart, wishlist and preferences, and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel, where enabled) to understand how the Website is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may then not work.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the Website and fulfil orders: hosting and database providers, payment gateways, courier and shipping partners, and email, SMS or WhatsApp delivery services
  • Law enforcement or government authorities when required by law
  • A successor business in the event of a merger, sale or transfer of assets

All service providers are required to protect your data in line with this policy.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your personal data (we may keep order records required by tax law)
  • Withdraw consent to marketing messages at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

The Website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
