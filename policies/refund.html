Refund & Return Policy

Truchilli products are food items made in small batches, so we handle returns carefully for everyone's safety. Please read the conditions below before placing an order.

Returns and replacements

No return or replacement will be accepted unless the product is damaged, defective, expired or missing. We accept a return or replacement request when:

  • The wrong product was delivered
  • An expired product was delivered
  • The product arrived damaged (torn, leaking or tampered packaging)
  • The order was incomplete and products were missing

When a request cannot be accepted

  • The product has been opened, used or altered
  • The original packaging (pouch, carton or labels) is missing
  • Damage or missing items are reported more than 2 days after delivery
  • The request is made more than 7 days after delivery
  • A missing-product claim without a video recorded before the package was opened
  • Taste or heat-level preference

How to raise a request

Within 2 days of delivery, send us your order number, a short description of the problem and clear photos (or the unboxing video for missing items) on WhatsApp at +91 81424 24269 or by email at thetruchilli@gmail.com. Logged-in customers can also use Report a problem under My account on the website.

We review every request within 2–3 business days and will either send a free replacement or issue a refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days (bank timelines may vary). For cash-on-delivery orders we will ask for your UPI ID or bank details.

Cancellations

An order can be cancelled free of charge until it is packed. Once dispatched it cannot be cancelled, but the conditions above still apply on delivery.
